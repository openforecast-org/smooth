DEFINITIONS
D1 lagged SSOE model: A pure additive single-source-of-error state space model $y_t = w' v_{t-l} + \epsilon_t$, $v_t = F v_{t-l} + g \epsilon_t$ with $k$ components, in which component $j$ of $v_{t-l}$ enters with its own lag $l_j$; its discount matrix is $D = F - g w'$ and its lag matrix is $L(B) = \mathrm{diag}(B^{l_1}, \dots, B^{l_k})$ for the backshift operator $B$; $\Lambda(\lambda) = \mathrm{diag}(\lambda^{l_1}, \dots, \lambda^{l_k})$ for a complex number $\lambda$.
D2 expanded discount matrix: The transition matrix $\tilde D$ of size $\sum_j l_j$ of the lag-1 system equivalent to $v_t = D v_{t-l}$, in which component $j$ carries itself and its $l_j - 1$ lagged copies; $\tilde F$, $\tilde g$ and $\tilde w$ are expanded in the same way, so that $\tilde D = \tilde F - \tilde g \tilde w'$.
D3 decaying weights: A lagged SSOE model has decaying weights when the weights $\tilde w' \tilde D^j \tilde g$ of the past observations in its fitted values tend to zero, that is, when every eigenvalue $\lambda$ of its expanded discount matrix that is reachable from $\tilde g$ and observable through $\tilde w$ has $|\lambda| < 1$; the largest such $|\lambda|$ is its largest mode modulus. A mode that $\tilde g$ does not reach but $\tilde w$ sees carries an initial state whose effect is not covered: with $|\lambda| > 1$ it grows.
D4 per-lag check: The test applied by smoothEigens() of smooth 4.6.0 and of its repository until commit 4a44c1c (src/headers/eigenCalc.h) to models without adaptive regressors: every eigenvalue of every diagonal block of $D$, one block per unique lag, has modulus below 1; reimplemented in numpy as per_lag_max().
D5 PBH reference: The largest modulus among the eigenvalues $\lambda$ of the expanded discount matrix for which the smallest singular values of $[\lambda I - \tilde D;\ \tilde w']$ and of $[\lambda I - \tilde D,\ \tilde g]$ both exceed $10^{-7}(\|\tilde D\|_2 + \|\tilde g\| + \|\tilde w\|)$ (Popov-Belevitch-Hautus rank tests), computed with numpy as pbh_max().
D6 cyclotomic cancellation: The procedure that computes the coefficients of $\theta(B) = \det(I - D L(B))$ and $\phi(B) = \det(I - F L(B))$ by an inverse discrete Fourier transform of their values at the $N$-th roots of unity ($N$ the smallest power of 2 above $\sum_j l_j$), and divides both by every cyclotomic polynomial $\Phi_n(B)$, $n$ dividing one of the lags, for as long as it divides both with a remainder at most $10^{-9}$ times the sum of absolute coefficients; the quotient of $\theta$ is the cancelled MA polynomial $\theta^*(B)$.
D7 step-down test: The Levinson-Durbin step-down (Schur-Cohn) recursion on $1 + c_1 B + \dots + c_n B^n$: for $j = n, \dots, 1$, $\kappa_j = c_j$ and $c_i \leftarrow (c_i - \kappa_j c_{j-i}) / (1 - \kappa_j^2)$; the test passes when every $|\kappa_j| < 1$, which holds if and only if all roots lie outside the unit circle (the $\kappa_j$ are the partial autocorrelations of the autoregression with this polynomial). [source B2]
D8 GUM: Generalised Univariate Model of the smooth package: a lagged SSOE model whose $F$, $g$ and $w$ are all estimated. [source B3]
D9 ETS(A,N,A): Exponential smoothing with additive error, no trend and additive seasonality of period $m$; here in the lagged form with a level of lag 1 and one seasonal state of lag $m$, $F = I$, $w = (1, 1)'$, $g = (\alpha, \gamma)'$. [source B1]

LIMITATIONS
L1 [analytic_flexibility; affects C1; reduces_transferability; mitigation partial] The 3042 ETS(A,N,A) models of the planned test of the per-lag check form the same fixed grid as exploratory run RN3, whose result prompted the hypothesis; only the 200 GUM models of that test were new data.
L2 [measurement; affects C1, C2, C3, C10, C11, C16; could_reverse; mitigation none] The PBH reference and the cyclotomic cancellation were written by the same agent and share the lag-1 expansion code and numpy's LAPACK eigenvalue routines; no independent implementation was compared.
L3 [sampling; affects C2, C3, C4, C5, C16; reduces_transferability; mitigation none] The random model sets (persistence ranges, the probabilities of zero persistence and of a constant, the standard deviations of the GUM matrices) were chosen by the agent and not drawn from models estimated on data.
L4 [measurement; affects C4, C5, C16; could_reverse; mitigation none] The reference decisions use numpy's polynomial roots (companion-matrix eigenvalues) for polynomials of degree up to 535 before cancellation with many roots near the unit circle, whose accuracy there was not checked by a second method.
L5 [other; affects C4, C5; reduces_transferability; mitigation none] The plans of the tests behind the step-down and bisection claims stated MA polynomial degrees up to 366; the generator code named in the plans, run unchanged, produced degrees up to 535 before cancellation.
L6 [other; affects C22; reduces_transferability; mitigation partial] The recursion of the counterexample to the monograph's condition was run for one parameter pair, with the initial states set to one and no data.
L7 [computational; affects C23; reduces_precision; mitigation none] The fit timings come from one machine, seven repeats per fit, with a run-to-run variation of about 10% between benchmark runs of the same code.
L8 [model_specification; affects C4, C16; reduces_transferability; mitigation none] For GUM models with structured parameters, such as a state with |F_ii| > 1 and zero persistence, theta and phi can share a factor with a root in |B| <= 1 that is not cyclotomic; the check keeps it and rejects the model although its observation weights decay, because the initial state of that mode has a growing effect.
L9 [measurement; affects C27; reduces_precision; mitigation none] Estimates with bounds="admissible" change for ETS+ARIMA models, whose admissible region changes, and the likelihood of such models can have several local optima: on ETS(A,N,A)+ARIMA(1,0,1) on AirPassengers the fit with the check of the whole model reached a lower log-likelihood than the fit with the check of the parts apart.
L10 [computational; affects C16, C26; could_bias_magnitude; mitigation addressed] Two defects found while testing were fixed before the planned runs: adam() with provided MA parameters added the MA coefficient of a lag without AR or differences twice to the persistence vector (and reapply() did so with the drawn ones, in R and Python), found by the structure check of the exploratory runs RN46 and RN47; and the Python build of smooth crashed at exit after checking a model whose MA polynomial has degree 16 or more (runs RN51 and RN52), because the caches of the check were freed through numpy after it had shut down. The second affected the implementation tested by C16 in Python, not its results. After the fixes the matrices built by the study matched those of adam() within 4.4e-15 (E21), and the planned run of the checkers (E22) exited normally.
L11 [computational; affects C16, C26; reduces_transferability; mitigation addressed] For an MA root exactly on the unit circle that does not cancel, the decision of the check is set by rounding: the checker P4 found three models of a wider generator, with values of the check between 0.99999999999 and 1, that the package accepted and the prototype rejected. Since this finding the bounds checkers admit a model only when the value is at most 1 - 1e-10 (decision DC8 of the lab record), which rejects these three; the planned tests ran before the margin, and their random models have no such root.
L12 [measurement; affects C24, C25; reduces_transferability; mitigation none] The PBH reference is not exact for a repeated unit root: the checker P4 found a model whose cancelled MA polynomial keeps a double root at -1, which the reference accepted and the cancellation and an exact gcd rejected. The random models of the planned tests have no such root.
